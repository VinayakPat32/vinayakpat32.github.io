1. Governing Equations

d²θ/dt² = −(g/L) sin(θ) − b (dθ/dt)

Exact nonlinear equation of motion integrated via 4th-order Runge-Kutta (RK4). The Ghost Pendulum replaces sin(θ) with θ.

2. Period & Divergence

T₀ = 2π √(L / g)

Small-angle approximation holds well for |θ₀| < 15°. At larger amplitudes, T ≈ T₀(1 + θ₀²/16 + …), causing the real pendulum to lag behind the ghost.

3. Mechanical Energy

KE = ½m(Lω)²  |  PE = mgL(1 − cos θ)

With zero damping (b = 0), total energy E = KE + PE is conserved and the phase-space portrait forms a closed orbit. Damping creates an inward spiral.

4. Forces & Lab Tips

T = m(g cos θ + Lω²)  |  Fg = mg

Drag the bob directly on the canvas to test large angles (e.g., 90° or 150°). Enable vectors to inspect how tension T exceeds gravity mg at the lowest point.
